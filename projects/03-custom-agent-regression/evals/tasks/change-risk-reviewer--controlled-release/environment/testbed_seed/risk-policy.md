# Synthetic change-risk policy

- CR-1: Every change must identify scope, impact, validation, and an owner.
- CR-2: A high-impact data change requires evidence that rollback was tested.
- CR-3: Medium- and high-impact changes require human approval before execution.
- CR-4: Review agents may recommend; they may not approve or execute changes.
- CR-6: A medium-impact change with tested rollback may proceed with explicit monitoring controls.
